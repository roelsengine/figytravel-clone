Peak Performance Training Plan

This programme is designed to enhance overall athletic performance by improving strength, endurance, speed, and coordination.

Start Training

01

Warm-Up & Mobility

Begin with dynamic stretches and light cardio to activate muscles, improve flexibility, and reduce the risk of injury.

02

Strength Development

Focus on compound exercises such as squats, lunges, push-ups, and deadlifts to build overall power and stability.

03

Endurance Training

Incorporate running, cycling, swimming, or circuit training to enhance cardiovascular fitness and stamina.

04

Speed & Agility Work

Use sprint intervals, ladder drills, and cone drills to improve quickness, coordination, and reaction time.

05

Cooldown & Recovery

End with light stretching, deep breathing, and hydration to promote recovery and prepare the body for the next session.
