Top 25 funding sources for volunteers in Tanzania UN Volunteers (UNV)

  • VSO (Voluntary Service Overseas) — Long-term placements and some funded/paid volunteer roles in Tanzania via programmes and partner-funded posts. vsointernational.org
  • Peace Corps (Tanzania) — US government volunteer programme that covers travel, housing and a living allowance for US citizens serving in Tanzania. peacecorps.gov
  • European Solidarity Corps / Erasmus+ (ESC) — EU-funded volunteering opportunities (sometimes fully funded) for eligible European youth — occasional Tanzania placements via partners. youth.europa.eu
  • Turing Scheme (UK) — UK government funding that can support student volunteering/internship placements overseas (applied via UK education providers). Good if you’re a UK student. GOV.UK
  • Projects Abroad – Scholarships and Financial Aid — Projects Abroad lists scholarships, grants and government funding routes for volunteers. Useful for students. projects-abroad.org
  • GoEco Volunteer Scholarships / Travel Grants — Operator-run scholarships and travel grants for selected volunteers. GoEco
  • World Endeavors – Financial Aid and Discounts — Private programme operator with financial-aid options and university-affiliated funding routes. World Endeavors
  • VolunteerForever / curated grants list — Big directory of volunteer scholarships, grants and travel-funding sources (great starting point to find niche grants). Volunteer Forever
  • Rotary Foundation (District and Global Grants) — Rotary clubs/districts fund volunteer-led projects or help individuals raise funds for service projects (requires club partnership). rotary.org rotary5040.org
  • Friends of Tanzania (FOT) — Project Grants — Small grants for community projects in Tanzania (useful if you’re running/partnering on a local project). fotanzania.org peacecorpsconnect.org
  • Tanzania Education Fund (TEF) — Hosts volunteers and (for longer placements) can provide a teacher-equivalent stipend — good for education placements. tanzaniaeducation.org
  • Volunteering Solutions – Fundraising and Scholarship Support — Operator offers fundraising assistance and guidance on external funding. volunteeringsolutions.com
  • GlobalGiving (and similar platforms) — Not a travel grant per se, but widely used to fundraise for project costs and support partner NGOs where volunteers work. globalgiving.org +1
  • Crowdfunding platforms (GoFundMe, FundMyTravel, Fundraiser tools) — Practical route for individuals to raise travel/program fees; many volunteers succeed with a good campaign. gofundme.com
  • fundmytravel.com International Volunteer HQ (IVHQ) / FundMyTravel partnership and fundraising tools — IVHQ and similar operators provide built-in fundraising support for participants. International Volunteer HQ
  • Mastercard Foundation (Scholars / youth programmes) — Large Africa-focused scholarship & youth employment programmes; useful for young Africans looking for funded study and training linked to community service. mastercardfdn.org
  • Commonwealth Foundation / Commonwealth youth grants — Grants and awards for youth-led projects in Commonwealth countries (Tanzania is a member) — can fund exchange/volunteer-type activities. Commonwealth Foundation Commonwealth
  • U.S. Embassy / Public Diplomacy Small Grants (Dar es Salaam) — Regular small grants for community projects and cultural exchange run by the US Embassy in Tanzania. Good if you partner a local CBO. tz.usembassy.gov
  • British Council (regional micro-grants and partnership funding) — Micro-grants and mobility grants (cultural/education projects) that can support exchange visits and volunteering partnerships. opportunities-insight.britishcouncil.org britishcouncilssa.grantplatform.com
  • PEPFAR / USAID small grants and local partner funds — Large donors sometimes run small-grant windows that local NGOs (and partnerships with volunteers) can access. Check the latest notices. uploads.mwp.mprod.getusinfo.com tz.usembassy.gov
  • Human Development Innovation Fund (HDIF) / Mawazo challenges (Tanzania) — Tanzania-focused fund supporting innovations and youth projects — can be relevant when volunteers bring project ideas. Wikipedia
  • University / study-abroad funding (institutional scholarships) — Many universities (via study-abroad office's) have travel grants, Turing/Erasmus channels or departmental funding for placements in Tanzania. See Projects Abroad / university pages. projects-abroad.org projects-abroad.co.uk
  • Local NGOs and partner mcro-grants — Local Tanzanian NGOs or community trusts sometimes provide stipends or cover volunteer costs when volunteers fill a technical or capacity gap (search local partners and ask). Example: Friends of Tanzania / local CBOs. fotanzania.org peacecorpsconnect.org
  • Corporate / employer volunteer grants ') — Many companies offer “employee volunteer grants” or will match funds you raise — check your employer’s CSR/HR programmes or use the VolunteerForever guide. Volunteer Forever
