About us

We are FIGY Travel Foundation - a Dutch volunteer travel organization based in Amsterdam and established to provide people with the opportunity to assist on a voluntary basis in teaching, healthcare and agriculture in the area of Mwanza, Tanzania, while using their skills and experiencing life in the local community in a safe environment.

FIGY Travel Foundation

FIGY Travel Foundation was founded to boost the resources of our partner (Stichting Support Schoolfees) beyond its existing sponsors to provide additional sources of income to allow it to extend its community work on the ground in the Sengerema District of Tanzania.

About us

Become a part of our community

At FIGY Travel Foundation , we enable people from all walks of life to have the opportunity to make a positive life-changing impact in rural communities in the beautiful African country of Tanzania through volunteering programs. Our programs are the most affordable and sustainable way to immerse yourself in new cultures, connect with like-minded international volunteers and make a difference through work and travel.

If you ever wanted to make a real impact in a time of need, this is your time and place to fill that gap on the mother continent. Volunteer in Tanzania at an affordable price with FIGY Travel, including accommodation in one of our beautiful lodges on the shores of Lake Victoria, Africa’s largest lake, meals, 24/7 in-country support, and more.

Visit the vast plains of the Serengeti national park with its awe-inspiring wildlife in our optional add-on safari experiences with local guides and drivers available at a much lower price than with the big commercial tour operators. End your stay in Tanzania with a vacation on the fascinating island of Zanzibar and visit the capital Stone Town, a historic Swahili coastal trading settlement and UNESCO world heritage site, as well as the stunning Indian Ocean golden beaches with their laid-back chill-out vibe.

The FIGY Philosophy

Become a part of our community

Fill the Gap Years Travel was founded to do what it says: ‘Fill the Gap,’ whether that is the year before, or after, studying at university, or the emotional gap of wanting to make a positive impact in the world; or bridging the cultural gap and stretching out a helping hand to those coming from a far less privileged background than yourself.

Our overwhelming priority is the safety of our volunteers and we do everything we can to ensure their security and comfort. Tanzania is generally a safe country for European travellers and FIGY Travel operates in a quiet rural area not far from the second city of Mwanza on the shores of Lake Victoria. Our people on the ground come from the communities in which we operate and support and this deep local knowledge and network is the best guarantee of safety we can provide.

Our icon is the fig tree, the ancient symbol of wisdom, strength, abundance and love, that is considered sacred across religions and cultures.

Making a lasting positive impact on communities in Tanzania

At FIGY Travel Foundation, our mission is to empower individuals to make a lasting positive impact on local communities in Tanzania through meaningful volunteer and travel experiences.

We partner with Stichting Support School Fees and small local suppliers to create sustainable change in the Sengerema District and support social projects in the Mwanza Region.

Creating meaningful volunteer experiences

We are dedicated to creating meaningful volunteer and travel experiences that make a positive impact on local communities in Tanzania.

Our team is passionate about supporting the work of our partner foundation and empowering individuals to make a difference in education (and in the near future also agriculture, and healthcare) in the Mwanza region.
