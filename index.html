Stage vrijwilligerswerk Tanzania

Bij FIGY.travel geloven we dat leren verder gaat dan het klaslokaal. Onze programma’s bieden studenten en stagiairs de kans om hun talenten in te zetten in lokale gemeenschappen, terwijl zij zelf waardevolle praktijkervaring opdoen. Samen werken we aan kennisuitwisseling, persoonlijke groei en duurzame ontwikkeling.

Note:Onze projecten zijn actief in de Mwanza-regio in Tanzania en richten zich op verschillende vakgebieden die direct bijdragen aan betere kansen voor jongeren en gemeenschappen.

Stage en vrijwilligers Projecten

Landbouw & Duurzaamheid
€ 1.974 (indicatie voor 6 weken) €329,- per week

In de landbouwprogramma’s ervaren deelnemers het rurale leven van dichtbij. Je werkt samen met lokale boeren en scholen aan duurzame landbouw, veeteelt en voedselprojecten die zowel onderwijs als inkomensgeneratie ondersteunen. Kennis delen en leren van elkaar staat centraal.

Onderwijs
€ 1.974 (indicatie voor 6 weken) €329,- per week

Onderwijs vormt de basis van elke sterke samenleving. Stagiairs ondersteunen scholen door samen te werken met lokale leerkrachten, lesprogramma’s te verrijken en creativiteit in de klas te brengen. Door sport, spel, muziek en debat help je leerlingen hun talenten te ontdekken en zelfvertrouwen op te bouwen.

Gezondheidszorg
€ 1.974 (indicatie voor 6 weken) €329,- per week

In de gezondheidszorg programma’s doen deelnemers praktijkervaring op in samenwerking met lokale zorgprofessionals. Je ondersteunt dispensaria, scholen en gemeenschapsactiviteiten gericht op welzijn, preventie en basiszorg, en draagt zo bij aan gezondere leefomstandigheden.

Feedback stagiares en vrijwilligers

Eva H.
Volunteer/internship


"My trip to Tanzania has been one of my best experiences in life"

"I got the opportunity from my school to do my internship in another country, and I was really interested in Tanzania. Me and three other students were introduced to FIGY Travel and Stichting Support School Fees (SSF), and we felt an instant connection. So, in October 2022 we stayed at Mwanza Lake View Farm for three months! We volunteered at the schools and visited a lot of places. Like a prison, some farms, Mwanza town, Juma island and many kinds of schools. We also did a lot of activities with the children from the school and from the neighborhood like games, puzzles, singing, dancing, sports, and we provided sexual education. This was a great experience, and I learned a lot from it. The people of Tanzania are so welcoming and friendly, it was amazing to meet all the teachers, children, and residents. The people that work at the farm are also so super friendly, they made me feel at home during my stay, so that was really nice. I felt very grateful to meet all these wonderful people.

Kikkie K
Volunteer/internship

"I would definitely recommend volunteering/doing an internship at this organization and I would love to come back again" 

For my pedagogy study internship, I came to Mwanza Lake View Farm in October 2022, where I stayed for three months. During these three months, I got to see a lot of the area and visited several schools. Some of these schools have already been renovated by FIGY's partner foundation: Stichting Support School Fees. This is a big step for the health and education of children, but also for the teachers. Thanks to Baraka (the Tanzanian co-owner of SSF), I was able to visit these schools and the surrounding area because he brought me here with his van. Besides Baraka, many other people work at Mwanza Lake View Farm. Everyone is very friendly and they give you a warm feeling as soon as you arrive. A lot is arranged for you if you want to get out and about, but if you want to stay at the Farm, that's certainly not a punishment either; the farm is beautifully situated on Lake Victoria, has a small beach and is nicely decorated with plants, besides that, the cottages are also fully equipped.
I would definitely recommend volunteering/doing an internship at this organization and I would love to come back again.


Dani Esquivel
